Our Journey

A quarter century of shaping engineers

Twenty-five years of nurturing innovators, building communities and touching countless lives. The Silver Jubilee is a moment to pause, to celebrate — and, most importantly, to look forward.

To honour this occasion we are running a year-long series of celebrations, programmes and initiatives under the banner of the Sahrdaya Silver Jubilee Celebrations 2026–27 — ambitious, impactful, and designed to involve every single member of the Sahrdaya family.

Beginning with the Grand Inauguration on Saturday, 26 September 2026, the celebrations continue right through the academic year and culminate in a spectacular three-day Grand Closing Ceremony in August 2027.

Saturday, 26 September 2026

The Grand Inauguration

The Silver Jubilee year opens at 10:00 AM on the Sahrdaya campus, Kodakara — with unveilings, launches and a live connection to our alumni across the world. This is just the beginning.
